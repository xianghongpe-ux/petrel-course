About PETREL AI PARTY

The PETREL AI PARTY is not a political party in the traditional sense. It is an open-source "Human-AI Collaborative Governance Operating System" — encompassing a constitution, AI models, governance algorithms, community tools, and educational courses, all with code and documentation publicly available. Its ultimate form is not an organization, but a protocol.

Why Decentralization Is Necessary

🔴 Single Point of Failure

Centralized: attack the HQ, servers, or core team and the whole system collapses
Protocolized: code and data are distributed across the network, no HQ to destroy

🟡 Trust Cost

Centralized: the public must trust "those who operate the AI hub"
Protocolized: code is open, algorithms are auditable — "Don't Trust, Verify"

🟠 Power Reversion

Centralized: whoever controls the hub controls all power
Protocolized: there is no hub to control — power is mathematically locked at the protocol layer

🟢 Propagation Bottleneck

Centralized: organizational growth depends on recruitment — linear
Protocolized: anyone can fork and replicate the protocol — exponential

Three Inviolable Red Lines

🚫 No Autonomous Decisions

AI outputs must be in "suggestion" format only; the protocol rejects any "command" format. Human final review cannot be bypassed.

🚫 No Self-Modification

Model updates require community proposal + multi-sig (≥5/9) + seven mandatory tests. Offline training, online inference.

🚫 No Bypassing Humans

All external outputs must carry human multi-sig credentials. Outputs without credentials are automatically rejected by the protocol layer.
